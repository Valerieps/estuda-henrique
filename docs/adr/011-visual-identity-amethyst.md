# ADR-011: Visual identity: Amethyst palette, Inter and Space Grotesk, 12px corners

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Decision:** The values of `src/ui/theme/tokens.css` change; no token is renamed. Colors follow the "Amethyst" direction (brand `#6d28d9` in the light theme, `#b69cff` in the dark one, violet-tinted neutrals, slate since the update below). Text is Inter 400 and 600, titles and big numbers are Space Grotesk 700 (new token `--font-display`, new `--font-weight-display`), all three bundled as latin-subset woff2 files in `public/fonts/` with their OFL licenses and declared by `@font-face` in `tokens.css`. `--radius` is 12px and `--radius-sm` 6px. A new color token, `--color-brand-soft` (the weakest line of the thermometer, notices), gets its text pairs in `tokens.test.ts`. `theme-color` in `index.html`, `public/manifest.webmanifest` and the two icons take the same brand and background.
**Why:** The owner picked this direction among the candidates (roadmap, "Identidade escolhida"). Violet on near-white and near-black passes WCAG AA in both themes, which `tokens.test.ts` checks. Only three font files (Inter 400 and 600, Space Grotesk 700, latin only, about 61 KB together) keep the first load light on a phone. Fonts are files under `'self'`, so the CSP needs no new origin (`docs/SEGURANCA.md`, ADR-002) and the app still talks to no CDN.
**Alternatives:** System font stacks only: no download, but titles lose the character the owner chose. A variable font file: one file for every weight, but larger than the three static subsets we use.
**Update (2026-11-14):** The neutrals move from violet-tinted to slate (blue-gray), in both themes: light `--color-bg` `#f1f4f8`, `--color-text` `#0f172a`, `--color-text-muted` `#475569`, `--color-border` `#e2e8f0`, `--color-border-strong` `#74819a`; dark `--color-bg` `#0d1117`, `--color-surface` `#161b22`, `--color-text` `#e6edf3`, `--color-text-muted` `#9aa6b2`, `--color-border` `#2a323d`, `--color-border-strong` `#6e7b8b`. The brand, its soft tint and the state colors stay. Why: the owner found the violet-tinted background pinkish and wanted a more professional look; she picked slate over neutral zinc, warm stone and near-white, shown on the Hoje screen in both themes. Slate also matches the gray of the "Novo" state, which was already from that family.
